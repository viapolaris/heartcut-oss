# The Paper Boat

A drawn story with no words.

A small boy, about 6, round face, messy brown hair, a green jumper too big for him. He folds a boat out of a page from his school notebook, writes his name on the side, and puts it in the stream behind his house after rain.

The boat floats away. He runs along the bank to follow it, past a wooden fence, under a low bridge, through tall reeds. A frog watches from a leaf. The boat spins in a small whirlpool and he holds his breath. It gets free.

The stream reaches a pond in a park. On the far side, a girl about the same age, red hair in a ponytail, a blue dress with white dots, picks the boat out of the water. She reads the name. She looks around and sees him across the pond. She waves.

The next morning there are two paper boats on the stream, side by side.

Soft and hopeful. Bright spring colours. Simple shapes.
